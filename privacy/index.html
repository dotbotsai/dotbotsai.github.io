dotBots Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Contact: hello@dotbots.app

This policy explains what the dotBots team collects when you use dotbots.app and Bot Battle, and why.

1. What we collect

  • Wallet address: to sign you in. You sign a message; no transaction, no fee.
  • Game data: your bot name, matches, results, XP and rankings.
  • X account (only if you link it): your handle and public account info, to verify Cup eligibility and dotBots Quest tasks. We can't post or change anything on your X account.
  • Security data: your IP address is used briefly to stop spam and abuse and is deleted within about an hour. For fair-play checks we keep only a one-way coded version, never the raw IP. Cloudflare Turnstile runs anti-bot checks.
  • Usage and error stats (PostHog): pages opened, errors and server response codes, with a random ID. No wallet, no name, and IP addresses are discarded.
  • Room chat stays in memory while the room is open and is not saved.
  • Emails you send to hello@dotbots.app.

2. Why we use it, and your consent

  • To run the game, rankings and Cup, to prevent cheating, spam and attacks, and to fix bugs.
  • To check Cup eligibility and dotBots Quest tasks through X, only if you choose to link it (a separate choice).
  • To answer your emails and meet legal duties.

By signing in, you agree to this use of your data. You can withdraw at any time by emailing hello@dotbots.app; we then delete your off-chain data, and you will no longer be able to play with that wallet. Withdrawing does not affect use before your request.

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions with legal effects on you. Fair-play reviews are checked by the team.

3. Security.

All traffic to dotbots.app and our game servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Our game database is encrypted at rest (AES-256) by our hosting provider, Cloudflare. Only the dotBots team can access it. No system is 100% secure, so keep your wallet and keys safe.

4. Sharing.

Service providers that run dotBots for us: Cloudflare (servers, database, Turnstile, email routing), PostHog (usage stats), GitHub (web hosting), Google (email inbox). They may process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own security and data protection terms. We may share data if the law requires it.

5. On your device.

The game saves progress and settings in your browser's local storage. We use no advertising cookies.

6. Blockchain data.

Wallets, NFTs and transactions are public by nature, and we can't change or delete them.

7. How long we keep it.

Testnet game data and coded security data are deleted at the testnet reset when mainnet starts. Data used to review Cup winners is kept only until prizes are settled. Emails are kept as long as needed to answer you. Our host keeps automatic database backups for up to 30 days, after which deleted data is gone from them too. Browser data stays until you clear it.

8. Your rights.

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete or export your off-chain data, or object to how we use it. Email hello@dotbots.app with your wallet address; we may ask you to sign a message to prove the wallet is yours. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Age.

dotBots is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if we learn we have, we delete it.

10. Changes.

We post changes on this page with a new date.

— The dotBots team
