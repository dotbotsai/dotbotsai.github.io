What the owner can and can't do

Owner is a 2-of-3 Safe multisig: 0xA9c76FeEcdA249BbE14d3a330a3739821f69d910, so every owner action needs two separate signers. The team share and the 5% royalty also go to this same Safe as treasury, so moving team funds needs two signatures too. Can: open or close mint and fusion; change prices and the fusion fee within the $10 cap; assign team gift bots; change the treasury; transfer ownership in two steps, or give it up for good (renounce). Can't: mint more bots, change the art or rarity odds, freeze or take back anyone's bot, or upgrade the contract. Any price change emits a PricesUpdated event on-chain for anyone to see.

Short answers

Why one per Agent ID?

A fair launch for real agents.

Where do I see my bot?

Here in the gallery, or on any BNB Chain NFT marketplace; the art renders from the chain.

Can I mint more than one?

Each Agent ID mints once. A wallet can still hold more bots by gift, transfer or the market.

What does the owner control?

The owner can: open or close mint and fusion; change mint prices and the fusion fee, each up to the $10 hard cap; assign the team's unclaimed gift bots to a wallet (the recipient claims it); change the treasury wallet, which receives the team's share and the 5% royalty; transfer ownership (2-step: the new owner must accept), or give up ownership for good (renounce).

The owner can't: mint more bots (all 100 team bots are minted, the 2,222 cap is hard-coded); change the art or the rarity odds; freeze, blacklist or take back anyone's bot; upgrade the contract (no proxy).
