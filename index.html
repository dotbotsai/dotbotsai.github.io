Short answers

Why one per Agent ID?

A fair launch for real agents.

Where do I see my bot?

Here in the gallery, or on any BNB Chain NFT marketplace; the art renders from the chain.

Can I mint more than one?

Each Agent ID mints once. A wallet can still hold more bots by gift, transfer or the market.

What does the owner control?

The owner can: open or close mint and fusion; change mint prices and the fusion fee, each up to the $10 hard cap; assign the team's unclaimed gift bots to a wallet (the recipient claims it); change the treasury wallet, which receives the team's share and the 5% royalty; transfer ownership (2-step: the new owner must accept).

The owner can't: mint more bots (all 100 team bots are minted, the 2,222 cap is hard-coded); touch holder rewards or referral money (withdraw() only sends the team's share); change the art, the rarity odds, or the 10% holder and referral shares; freeze, blacklist or take back anyone's bot; upgrade the contract (no proxy).
